## One thing to flag

A video intro + 10-second forced loading screen adds real time before a visitor can even see the offer or hit "Buy Now." For cold Meta ad traffic, every extra second before the CTA is visible tends to increase drop-off — so it's worth deciding whether the full video should play every visit, or whether it should be shown once per session (e.g. skipped on repeat visits/page reloads using a simple session flag), so returning visitors or people re-clicking the ad aren't forced to sit through the whole sequence again.
