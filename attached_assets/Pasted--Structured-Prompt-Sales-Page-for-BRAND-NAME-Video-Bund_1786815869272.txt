## 3. Page Structure (in order)

1. **Loading screen** — placeholder for my existing visual asset, auto-transitions into hero after load or after animation completes
2. **Hero section**
   - Headline: outcome-driven, not feature-driven (e.g. "15,000+ [Niche] Videos. One Download. $9.")
   - Subheadline: who it's for + what problem it solves
   - Visual: video preview / auto-playing muted looping sample clip or grid of thumbnails
   - Primary CTA button: **"Buy Now for $9"** — sticky/repeated at multiple scroll points
3. **What's included** — breakdown of the 15,000+ videos (categories, formats, resolution, licensing/usage rights)
4. **Why this matters / problem-solution** section — short, scannable, not paragraph-heavy
5. **Social proof** — testimonials, use-case examples, or "X people bought this" style trust signal ([INSERT REAL TESTIMONIALS/PROOF — do not fabricate])
6. **Urgency/scarcity element** (optional but effective) — e.g. "Price increases after [date]" or limited bonus — must be **truthful**, Meta disapproves ads/pages with fake countdowns or false scarcity
7. **FAQ** — cover licensing, file delivery method, refund policy, "will this work for [use case]"
8. **Final CTA block** — repeat the offer + button
9. **Footer** — Refund Policy, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Contact email (all **required** — Gumroad and Meta both expect these to exist, and Meta will reject/restrict ad accounts without them)

---

## 4. Meta Pixel Integration (critical — build this in explicitly)

- Install **Meta Pixel base code** in `<head>`, fires `PageView` on load
- Fire `ViewContent` when the hero/offer section is visible
- Fire `InitiateCheckout` on the "Buy Now" button click (before the Gumroad overlay opens)
- **Purchase event**: since Gumroad handles the actual payment, the Pixel can't natively see the transaction. Handle this by:
  - Setting a **custom "Thank You" redirect URL** in Gumroad's product settings, pointing to a `/thank-you` route on this same site
  - Firing the `Purchase` event (with value: 9, currency: USD) on that thank-you page load
  - Note this in the build so the AI scaffolds a `/thank-you` route, not just a single page

---

## 5. Gumroad Overlay Integration

- Use Gumroad's official overlay/embed JS script (not a redirect to Gumroad's hosted page) so checkout stays frictionless and doesn't break the ad funnel experience
- "Buy Now" button triggers the Gumroad overlay directly
- Confirm the Gumroad product's **custom thank-you URL** is set to the site's `/thank-you` route (see above)

## 6. Compliance Notes (important for account survival)

- Meta Ads will reject/restrict accounts for: misleading claims, fake countdowns/scarcity, missing policy pages, "too good to be true" pricing language without substantiation
- Make sure Refund Policy, Terms, and Privacy Policy pages actually exist and are linked in the footer, not just mentioned
- Keep all claims about the video bundle **factually accurate** — no invented testimonials or fake stats
